# 道初 更多遗物

An independent Slay the Spire relic mod by 沛孤, built against the locally installed ModTheSpire 3.30.0 and BaseMod 5.56.0.

Relics (23 total; 8 common, 8 uncommon, 7 rare):

- Tight Knot: Every 3rd card played each turn grants 3 Block.
- Cracked Wax Seal: The first card Exhausted each turn grants 3 Block.
- Inverted Hourglass: The first shuffle each combat grants 1 Energy and draws 2 cards.
- Fine Copper Chain: After you play a Skill, your next Attack deals 4 additional damage.
- Worn Bandage: The first time you become Bloodied each combat, heal 4 HP.
- Empty Flask: Whenever you use a Potion, gain 1 Strength.
- Ash Pouch: Whenever you Exhaust a Status or Curse, deal 6 damage to ALL enemies.
- Patched Leather: At Rest Sites, heal 4 additional HP when you Rest.
- Loose Button: At the end of your turn, if you have at least 5 cards in hand, gain 4 Block.
- Bent Nail: After you play a 0-cost Attack, it deals 5 additional damage.
- Folded Fan: If you play no Attacks during a turn, draw 2 additional cards at the start of your next turn.
- Sealed Letter: Whenever you obtain a Power, gain 10 Gold.
- Copper Bell: The first time you kill an enemy each combat, draw 2 cards.
- Waxed Thread: Every 3rd card you manually discard gives you 1 Energy.
- Rusty Compass: Whenever you enter an Event room, gain 15 Gold.
- Paperweight: At the start of your turn, if your draw pile has 5 or fewer cards, gain 1 Energy.
- Spare Key: Whenever you open a non-Boss Chest, obtain a random Potion.
- Golden Thread: The first time you gain 10 or more Block at once each turn, gain 1 Energy.
- Clockwork Feather: The first time your draw pile becomes empty each combat, draw 2 cards.
- Cinder Crown: Every 3rd card you Exhaust gives you 1 Strength.
- Archivist's Seal: Card Rewards contain 1 additional card.
- Lead Counterweight: At the start of each combat, gain 2 Strength. At the start of your first turn, lose 1 Energy.
- Empty Dice Cup: If you end your turn with no cards in hand, gain 1 Energy and draw 1 card at the start of your next turn.

The mod only depends on BaseMod. It does not modify or depend on AKDream's More Relics.
